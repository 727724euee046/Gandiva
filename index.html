WHO WE ARE

GANDIVA RACING

Engineering Speed. Driving Innovation.

Gandiva Racing is a student-driven Formula Kart and electric vehicle racing team. Our team brings together passionate engineers, designers, innovators, and motorsport enthusiasts to design, manufacture, test, and race high-performance electric karts.

Inspired by the legendary Gandiva bow, which represents power, precision, and excellence, we bring the same values to engineering and motorsport.

🏆 3-Time EKVC Champions

Gandiva Racing is proud to be a three-time EKVC Champion. Our success represents our commitment to engineering excellence, innovation, teamwork, and performance.

OUR VISION

To become a leading student motorsport team by creating innovative and high-performance electric vehicles.

OUR MISSION

To design, build, test, and race competitive electric karts while developing future engineers.

OUR STRENGTH

Engineering excellence, teamwork, innovation, determination, and passion for motorsport.

TEAM FOUNDED - 2023

THE BEGINNING OF GANDIVA RACING

Gandiva Racing was founded in 2023 by a group of passionate engineering students from Sri Krishna College of Engineering and Technology.

The team's vision was to design, manufacture, and race high-performance electric karts while developing technical knowledge, teamwork, and leadership skills.

FIRST EKVC CHAMPIONSHIP - 2023

FIRST CHAMPIONSHIP

Gandiva Racing won its first EKVC Championship through excellent engineering, teamwork, and outstanding racing performance.

SECOND EKVC CHAMPIONSHIP - 2024

SECOND CHAMPIONSHIP

After improving the vehicle design, Gandiva Racing successfully defended its EKVC title and became two-time champions.

THIRD EKVC CHAMPIONSHIP - 2025

THREE-TIME CHAMPIONS

Gandiva Racing achieved its third EKVC Championship, proving its excellence in innovation, engineering, and motorsport performance.
